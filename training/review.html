What to label
  1. Box identifiable discarded items such as wrappers, bottles, cups and plastic bags.
  2. Include every visible litter item you can identify. Use separate boxes when items can be distinguished. For tiny touching pieces that cannot be separated, use one tight box around the cluster.
  3. Leave out leaves, shadows, road markings, bins and carried shopping bags.
  4. Skip views where blur, darkness or ambiguous material prevents you from identifying litter. Explain why.
